# Combat extras

> Five-minute read. Bullets over prose. See [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Builds on [combat-tracker](combat-tracker.md) (CBT); sprint plan [sprint-1](../agents/sprint-1.md) track D (#177).

## 1. Summary

- Combat depth on top of the MVP tracker: **effects** (5e conditions, custom round-based effects like "Bless: 10 rounds", **concentration**), **temp HP**, **death saves**, **add combatants mid-fight**, and two token overlays: **HP bar** and **condition icons**.
- Every capability is a server command ([ADR-0016](../architecture/adr/0016-domain-events-pending-actions.md)). Rules (expiry, concentration prompts, death-save failures, temp HP absorption) run **inside the causing command's transaction** as rule hooks and the HP rules helper; no post-commit listener writes state ([ADR-0035](../architecture/adr/0035-combat-rule-hooks.md), accepted #178). A crash loses nothing.
- Players see what the table sees: conditions and effects on visible creatures, their own PC's numbers, a monster's health band only (#138), other PCs' death state but no HP.
- **Sprint 2, phase 1** (DM notes #188 + session-sim-4; FR-CBTX-44–50): the Add dialog rolls initiative on the way in, creates tokens (hidden by default) at the centre of the DM's view and offers average / rolled HP (N5); a token-less combatant can be **linked** to a token dropped later, which fixes missing movement and targeting for added creatures (N6, N6.1); chips use the room they have (G1); death-save lines no longer show counters (G7); concentration cards show in Initiative and on the DM's bell, close as "Closed", and cue the player's token on the DM map (G9, N11); a dead or downed PC keeps its map cue after End (item 14).
- **Sprint 4** (track R, DM [#243](../decisions-log.md) [19]; FR-CBTX-51–53): **trigger badges**: Initiative rows show the rule triggers in effect (Divine Fury, Sneak Attack) with their state (ready / used this turn / uses left) beside the effect chips, tokens show one trigger icon with the list on hover; data from MOD's `trigger-state` ([FR-MOD-53–55](effect-modifiers.md#6-functional-requirements)). A new append-only effect-changed registry (seams S6, in or out of combat; also fog-of-war's Blinded line) keeps "in effect" right when an effect starts or ends ([ADR: trigger state](../architecture/adr/0049-trigger-state.md), accepted at sprint-4 gate 1, #245). Badges go to the DM and the owner only (OQ-MOD-40, -41 decided #245). Test plan: [MOD §13.1](effect-modifiers.md#131-sprint-4-track-r-mod-cbt-cbtx-charx).
- **Sprint 5, track W** (FR-CBTX-54–56): trigger chips shrink to icons before anything folds, so Divine Fury stays visible next to "Rage 10r"; Effect… starts with a source players could see; the token menu has one Invisible (fog's). Client only, 0 B.
- **Sprint 6, track WF** (FR-CBTX-57, -58): every part beside a name (hidden icon, Ally / Enemy chip, effect and trigger chips) moves to a second line before the name is cut; a trigger chip shows its whole label or just ⚡ + uses, never "Searing Str…". Client only, 0 B.

## 2. Scope

Accepted by the DM (#178, all picks; sprint plan #177).

- **Effects**: 15 conditions (5e list incl. exhaustion 1–6), custom effects with a label and optional duration in rounds, concentration (one per creature). Effects on monsters (combatant) and PCs (character; persist outside combat, OQ-CBTX-05).
- **Durations**: round-based, end at the start of the anchor's turn; auto-expire on Next, restored on Prev (OQ-CBTX-02/04). Effects linked to a concentration end with it.
- **Concentration**: on damage, a **concentration card** (notification with action "Roll save", DC = max(10, ⌊damage / 2⌋)); click-to-roll CON save; fail → concentration and linked effects end.
- **Temp HP**: PCs and monsters; absorbs damage first; undo-safe.
- **Death saves** (PCs at 0 HP): successes / failures, nat 1 = 2 failures, nat 20 = 1 HP, 3 successes = stable, 3 failures = dead; damage at 0 HP = 1 failure (crit 2); DM stabilise.
- **Add combatants mid-fight**: tokens from the map, characters or monsters from a picker; initiative per [FR-CBT-46/47](combat-tracker.md#6-functional-requirements) (#172).
- **Token overlays**: HP bar (DM: all; player: own PC proportional; monsters a band-tone bar, no proportion, #178), condition / effect icons, PC death badge.
- **UI**: Initiative rows and menus (DM, player), token menu entries, notification views, and three sheet sections registered through character-sheet-full's seam (§7.1).

## 3. Goals

- The DM runs conditions, concentration and durations without paper: nothing to remember at turn start, a concentration save prompt on every hit.
- A dying PC's player rolls their own death saves from the sheet; everyone sees who is dying, stable or dead.
- A wrong apply is still fixed in two clicks (Undo): temp HP, death-save failures and effects ended by that damage come back too.
- Hard kill at any point → restart → effects, temp HP, death saves, pending concentration cards intact.
- No hidden creature, secret effect, monster HP or temp HP reaches a player.

## 4. Non-goals

- Rules automation beyond §6: no advantage / disadvantage from conditions, no speed 0 from grappled, no auto-crit vs paralysed, no condition immunities (reminders only, OQ-CBTX-06).
- Undo of effect, temp HP or death-save commands (corrected by the inverse command; undo stays combat log entries only, ADR-0016, OQ-CBTX-20).
- Sheet layout and editing (→ [character-sheet-full](character-sheet-full.md), CHARX). Spells and slots (CHARX).
- Items in §16.

## 5. User stories

- **DM:** As the DM, I want to box-select three goblins and mark them Frightened from the token menu, and see the icons on their tokens, so that I don't track conditions on paper.
- **DM:** As the DM, I want Bless to drop off Mira and Thorn when Mira loses concentration or after 10 rounds, so that I never forget an expired buff.
- **DM:** As the DM, I want to drop two more goblins on the map mid-fight, add them to combat and roll their initiative, so that reinforcements cost seconds.
- **Player:** As a player, when my concentrating cleric is hit, I want a prompt with the DC and one click to roll my CON save, so that nobody has to remember.
- **Player:** As a player at 0 HP, I want to roll my death save on my sheet and see my successes and failures, so that the drama stays at the table, not in arithmetic.
- **Player:** As a player, I want to set my temp HP after False Life and see hits eat it first.

## 6. Functional requirements

All commands: rows + event-log entry + domain event in one transaction; IDs, time from `ctx`, dice from `ctx.dice` ([AGENTS §9.3](../../AGENTS.md#93-fixtures), #137). Failure → no change, event or log entry. "Started encounter" = the campaign's `started` encounter ([FR-CBT-07](combat-tracker.md#6-functional-requirements)). Target ref = `{combatantId}` (an active combatant of the started encounter; a PC combatant resolves to its character) or `{characterId}` (any character of the campaign).

### 7.1 Character sheet seam (CHARX boundary)

Settled by the orchestrator (D/S seam). CBTX owns data, rules, intents and visibility; CHARX owns the sheet layout and the section registry: `src/client/common/features/character-sheet/sheet-sections.ts`, `export const sheetSections: readonly SheetSection[]`, append-only, created empty by CHARX. Entry `{id "<prefix>.<name>", slot: "header" | "vitals" | "main" | "tab", order, title?, clients?, kinds?, available?(store, creatureId), component: ComponentType<{creatureId, client}>}`; components read the store and send their own intents. CBTX registers three entries, all `kinds: ["character"]`, both clients:

| Section | Slot | `available` | Shows | Intents | Who may call |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cbtx.temp-hp` | `vitals` | always | Temp HP next to HP (`entity.tempHp`); number input + Set; hint "Temp HP doesn't stack — keep the higher" | `cbtx.setTempHp {target: {characterId}, value}` | owner, DM |
| `cbtx.death-saves` | `vitals` | `deathState != null` | 3 success + 3 failure pips, state (Dying / Stable / Dead); **Death save** roll control (Shift / Ctrl, physical dice, DM public toggle); DM: Stabilise, Set… | `cbtx.rollDeathSave` (owner, DM); `cbtx.setDeathSaves` (DM) | as listed |
| `cbtx.effects` | `header` | always | Chip row of active effects on this character: conditions (+ level), custom with "N rounds left", concentration ("Concentrating: Bless"). - **Play state vs sheet data:** `tempHp`, `deathSuccesses`, `deathFailures` are CBTX-owned play-state columns on `entity` (like `hpCurrent`), never in `statBlock` nor in CHARX's `creature-sheet`; `PUT /api/entities` never touches them. Effects live in CBTX's `effect` table. CHARX models no temp HP, death-save or condition fields. Its summary card for other players may show "Down" / `deathState` only (FR-CBTX-32).
- **Fields consumed from the sheet side:** `entity.hpCurrent`, `statBlock.hpMax`, and CHARX's save resolver for the CON save bonus (FR-CBTX-42).
- **Sheet HP control:** the sheet keeps `char.adjustHp`; CBTX wires it through `hpRules.apply` (temp HP absorbs, death-save failures at 0 HP, concentration cards). The sheet's "Down" badge may read `deathState`.
- **Lock:** these sections are play state; CHARX's sheet lock ([character-sheet-full](character-sheet-full.md) FR-CHARX-12) does not apply, as for HP.
- Until the section registry merges, the DM Initiative row and token menu cover every control; players get the sections when it lands.

## 11. Edge cases & failure modes

- **Player disconnect / reconnect:** a concentration card waits pending; summary toast on return (FR-NOTE-15); the DM may roll it meanwhile. Chips, bars, pips rebuild from the snapshot; nothing replays.
- **DM disconnect:** players still roll death and concentration saves; expiry needs Next, so nothing expires while the DM is away.
- **Sprint 4 (badges):** reconnect → badges from the snapshot's `trigger-state` rows. An effect started, ended or restored by any path (in or out of combat) runs the effect hooks in that transaction (FR-CBTX-51), so a crash leaves effect and badge consistent. Prev / Next / Undo: MOD rewrites the state (FR-MOD-54). Other players never get the rows, so hiding a token changes nothing for them. Owner reassignment moves the rows (FR-MOD-54). Chips never grow the row (FR-CBTX-46 fitting).
- **Sprint 5 (W):** chip fitting and the Effect… default are client-side reads of the store: reconnect / snapshot → re-measured and re-derived, nothing stored; a server crash changes nothing. The turn combatant revealed or hidden while the dialog is open: the default is taken when the dialog opens, the DM's later pick is kept.
- **Server crash:** every rule runs in its command's transaction: apply ⇔ temp ⇔ death counters ⇔ concentration card ⇔ effects ended; Next ⇔ expiry; undo ⇔ restores. Restart → identical snapshots. No post-commit work.
- **Concurrent:** two tabs roll one card → second `conflict`. Player rolls a death save while the DM heals → serial: the roll after the heal → `conflict` "Not dying". Concentration ends (save failed) while a second card is open → that card closes `ended` in the same transaction.
- **Prev / Next races:** `expected*` guards (FR-CBT-14). Next + Prev restores exactly the effects that Next expired.
- **Undo** after a later sheet heal or death save: inverse deltas, clamped (I2); temp restore clamped; death counters by delta, so later rolls survive (FR-CBTX-22). **Restore into a taken slot** (undo or Prev reactivating a concentration or condition the target has again): skipped and named in `notRestored`; the command still succeeds.
- **Removed combatant:** effects stay (shown with Out); expiry still runs. **Token deleted / unbound:** effects stay on the combatant; no icon.
- **Added mid-fight to a hidden token:** hidden combatant; its effects `hidden`. Reveal → token hook upserts both.
- **Leaks accepted:** the band-tone bar (#178; players had the band); a PC's `deathState` (players see Down already, #154); the rolling player's own card shows damage dealt (#138) and a concentration card names the DC (owner only); effect chips on visible monsters ("Goblin: Bless") reveal what the party saw.
- **Invalid input:** strict zod; labels as text; a player can't target another PC, a monster, or send `public`.
- **Sprint 2.** *Add with tokens:* tokens, combatants, rolls and ranks commit in one transaction (crash → none or all; restart → identical snapshots). Hidden created tokens and their combatants never reach players. A mistaken add: Remove, then delete its tokens ([FR-CBT-68](combat-tracker.md#6-functional-requirements)). *Link:* two DM tabs linking the same token → the second gets `conflict` (token already bound) or `invalid` (combatant no longer token-less); reconnect re-derives the menu from the store. *Chips* re-measure on every render; nothing stored. *Cue* is live-only (FR-NOTE-30). *Death-save lines:* rows from before keep their counters (no backfill; accepted).
- **Leaks accepted (sprint 2):** setting a monster's temp HP still sends players a `combatant` upsert with no visible change (sprint-1 leftover): a timing tell only, as entities' accepted same-redaction upsert (entities §9); until live-sync's hub-level skip lands (OQ-CBTX-25, decided #190; owner: integrator). A linked token's combatant takes the token's name, which players may see change (as a token rename).
- **Accepted limits (sprint 2, review P2a-10):** rolled HP has no re-roll (the DM edits HP with Damage / heal; the roll entry stays); tokens created at Start or by an add are ordinary tokens and stay on the map after End, like placed ones; a wrong link is undone with Unlink (FR-CBTX-45).

## 12. Performance & bandwidth

- `effect` ≈ 300 B (DM), ≈ 200 B (player); cap 300 active per campaign → ≤ 90 KB DM snapshot worst, typ. < 5 KB. CON-44 heavy DM snapshot 491 KB + 5 KB.
- One `TurnChanged` frame grows by the expired rows (typ. 0–3 × 300 B). An apply adds ≤ 1 card per target (≤ 20 × 1.5 KB worst). Bulk condition on 20 tokens = one event ≤ 6 KB.
- Overlays are pure, per token per render; `bar` + `icons` add ≤ 6 Konva nodes per token; 60 active tokens stay within the canvas budget.
- Sprint 4: the trigger icon is one of the existing ≤ 4 icons (no node budget change); each `effectHooks` line costs one indexed read of the listed creatures' effects and triggers per command. Badge data is MOD's `trigger-state` (measured: DM +22,326 B realistic, +84,102 B worst; players ≤ 2,592 B; [MOD §12](effect-modifiers.md#12-performance--bandwidth)); CBTX adds no bytes.
- Sprint 2: an add with 20 instances = one event with ≤ 20 token creates, ≤ 40 DM-only roll entries (≤ ~25 KB DM, ≤ ~8 KB players); `CombatantBound` ≤ 1 KB. Chip measuring is one ResizeObserver per window. No new record type; CON-44 re-run (created tokens count in scenes-map's budget).
