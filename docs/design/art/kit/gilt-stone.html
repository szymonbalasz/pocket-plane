What to check

The round-01 A skin and round-02 sheet built for real: browser fonts, CSS frames, SVG corners. Every size here is the size it will be in the app.

  • Switch the type pairing in the top bar. Titles and labels change; numbers and body follow the pairing.
  • Hover or tab to any boxed number, roll button or condition icon. The scroll tooltip unrolls after the shared 400 ms delay.
  • The narrow sheet is a true 320 × 440 window, today's default. Compare it with the enlarged one beside it.
  • Button states, gem close, tabs and the active tool. Compact rows come from concept B, the tool highlight and Game log from C.
  • New in draft 3: the 48 px top bar and launcher, minimise buttons, the toast stack and damage card, the sheet's Actions tab, menus, dialog, popover, toggle tip, table, slider and loading states. Every shared component the app uses now has a look.
